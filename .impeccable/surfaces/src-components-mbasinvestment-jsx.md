# Surface: MBAS Investment Limited home page

Mode: Persuade. Audiences weighted equally: partners/government contacts (often older) and Nigerian tutor applicants. Actions: contact the company; reach the Zoho tutor application. Proof on hand: sectors, SLING Education partnership, tutor requirements, contact details. No metrics, no testimonials (see PRODUCT.md).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is the company's annual report in black cloth and gold foil: a cover, a contents page, then chapters. It refuses the dark-SaaS template of glowing cards, blurred orbs, badges and hero stats.

OWN-WORLD: Black bookcloth ground (#0D0C0A) in three tonal steps, hairline rules in dark bronze, gold foil (#C8A24B) used only for rules, the emblem, numerals and the primary action; warm off-white ink. Libre Caslon Display for chapter titles, Atkinson Hyperlegible Next for everything read. Ruled schedules and definition lists instead of cards. Square corners, 2px radius at most.

STORY: A partner sees a steady, legitimate Abuja company across three sectors and finds the phone number without searching. A tutor sees the vacancy notice, checks the requirements in plain lists and applies in one click.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Report cover. Left 7/12: Caslon headline at ~80px, one plain sentence, two actions (gold "Work with us", outlined "Apply to teach English"). Right 5/12: double-ruled foil frame holding the shield emblem above a Contents list with leader rules linking every chapter. Imprint line across the bottom naming the three sectors.

FORM: Annual report / prospectus, my ranked #1 (IMPECCABLE'S PICK), seed 6832cfcf. Code-led. Benchmark: Mercury. Signature motion: foil stamping, where gold rules draw in and one sheen passes across the emblem on load; each chapter's rule draws as it enters. Nothing loops, and reduced motion shows everything static.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
